You ask Claude for an email and it comes back close to your brand. The green is a shade off. The small label above the headline is too faint to read. The buttons look right in Figma, then shift when someone rebuilds the email in Klaviyo.

Claude can design on-brand emails. Reliable Claude email design comes down to three things: your brand written down for email, a Figma file built from your own blocks, and a second Claude session that reviews each email blind before anyone builds it.

I looked for those on GitHub and found almost nothing for email, so I built them and put them there for free: a brand file template called EMAIL.md, a Figma setup checklist, the Claude skills that design and review each email, and a full example brand with 4 finished emails. Here's what's in it and what happened when I tested it.
